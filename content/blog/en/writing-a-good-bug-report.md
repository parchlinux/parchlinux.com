A useful bug report does not have to be long. It needs enough information for another person to understand the failure and, ideally, reproduce it.

## Use a specific title

Describe what failed and where. A precise title also makes duplicate reports easier to find.

## List reproduction steps

1. Where you started.
2. What action you took.
3. What you expected.
4. What happened instead.

## Include the exact error

Put logs or terminal output in a code block and remove only information that is genuinely sensitive.
